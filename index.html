How These Apps Handle Your Privacy

1. No Accounts or Personal Info

You don't need to sign up, log in, or hand over your name, email, phone number, or location to use any of these apps. Everything works right out of the box.

2. Everything Stays on Your Device

Your settings, notes, recipes, battery logs, and session history are saved locally on your phone (using Android DataStore, SharedPreferences, or a local SQLite/Room database). Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly hit export or share something yourself.

3. Zero Ads, Zero Trackers

There are no ad networks (like AdMob or Unity Ads) and no analytics or crash-tracking SDKs (like Firebase Analytics or Mixpanel). I don't track how you use the apps or build profiles on anyone.

4. Open Source

The source code for my open-source apps is up on GitHub (under GPLv3), so anyone who's curious can inspect the code and verify that it actually does what this page says.

5. Kids' Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

None of these apps collect personal information from anyone—including children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

6. Questions?
