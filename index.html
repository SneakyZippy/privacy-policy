General Privacy Principles Across All Apps

1. No Account or Personal Identity Collection

None of our applications require you to register an account, log in, or submit personal identifiers (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical location). You can use all features anonymously and independently.

2. Local-First Processing and Storage

Application data—including preferences, diagnostic records, mindful session logs, and custom configuration—is stored exclusively on your device (using Android DataStore, SharedPreferences, or local SQLite/Room databases). This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an export or share action via the system share sheet.

3. No Advertising & No Analytics Trackers

Our applications contain zero third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity, Meta Audience Network) and zero analytics/tracking libraries (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel). We do not profile users, track usage across devices, or sell browsing habits.

4. Open Source Transparency

The full source code for our applications is hosted publicly on GitHub under open-source licenses (GPLv3). Anyone is free to inspect, audit, compile, and verify that the codebase strictly conforms to these privacy guarantees.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Our applications do not collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

6. Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy policies or open-source software, please reach out via:
