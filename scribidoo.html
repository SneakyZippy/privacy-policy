Scribidoo

Package ID: com.scribidoo.app
Offline handwritten note-taking, PDF annotation, and voice-memo canvas workspace.

Effective Date: November 2026 Application Version: 1.0+ Developer: SneakyZippy Source Code: GitHub Repository

Summary: Scribidoo operates 100% offline on your device. The app does not declare internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested), collects no personal information, and keeps all notes, PDFs, and voice recordings strictly on your device.

1. Overview

Scribidoo is a digital notebook and canvas app for stylus handwriting, PDF markup, and embedded voice memos. Your notes, sketches, documents, and recordings are personal—Scribidoo is built to work completely offline with zero external servers.

2. Information Collection and Processing

Scribidoo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

  • No Internet Permission: Scribidoo cannot connect to the internet or transmit data off your device.
  • No Accounts: No login, registration, or cloud account is required.
  • No Telemetry or Ads: Zero analytics libraries, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs are included.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Permission / Feature Purpose Data Treatment
RECORD_AUDIO
(Microphone)		 Allows you to record optional voice memos directly onto a note canvas using the in-app voice recorder. Audio is recorded only while you actively start and run a recording. Audio files are saved exclusively in your device's local app storage and are never uploaded or transcribed externally.
FileProvider
(Document Export & Sharing)		 Enables exporting canvases or annotated documents (e.g., PDF/image exports) through Android's system share sheet. Triggered strictly by your explicit export action. You choose where exported files are saved or shared.

4. Local Storage and Data Retention

All notebooks, ink strokes, imported PDFs, voice memo audio files, and canvas preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage directory on your device. Deleting a note or uninstalling Scribidoo removes the associated local files from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Scribidoo does not collect any data whatsoever from any users, including children under 13 years of age.
