🍳 App-Specific Privacy Policy

MadeWithLove

Package ID: com.madewithlove
Local-first recipe manager, cookbook, grocery list, and cooking timer companion.

Effective Date: November 2026 Application Version: 1.3+ Developer: SneakyZippy Source Code: GitHub Repository

Summary: MadeWithLove stores your recipes, meal plans, and grocery lists locally on your device. Network access is used strictly on-demand when you explicitly import a recipe or image from a URL you provide. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no trackers, and no advertisements.

1. Overview

MadeWithLove is a recipe and cooking companion app designed to help you collect, scan, organize, and cook recipes without accounts, paywalls, or tracking. All personal cookbook data lives in a local SQLite Room database on your device.

2. Information Collection and Network Usage

MadeWithLove does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

  • No Accounts or Cloud Sync: You never need to create an account or sign in. Your recipes are never uploaded to a SneakyZippy server.
  • On-Demand Web Recipe Import: When you paste a recipe URL or image URL to import it, the app fetches only that specific webpage or image directly from the website you requested (using HTTP/HTTPS or an on-device WebView fallback for sites that require browser rendering).
  • On-Device Photo OCR: When scanning a recipe from a photo, text recognition is performed locally on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition without uploading your photos to external servers.
  • Zero Analytics or Ads: MadeWithLove contains no analytics SDKs, crash telemetry, or advertising libraries.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Permission / Feature Purpose Data Treatment
INTERNET Fetches recipe pages (JSON-LD / HTML) or recipe photos from URLs that you explicitly enter in the recipe importer. Used solely for user-initiated imports. No background network requests, telemetry, or analytics are sent.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers local cooking timer alerts and step reminders while you are preparing a recipe. Scheduled and dispatched locally on your device via Android's notification manager.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic alerts when a cooking timer completes. Local hardware actuation only.
WAKE_LOCK Ensures cooking timer alarms and reminders fire reliably while a timer is running. Released immediately when the timer completes or is cancelled.
FileProvider
(User-Initiated Sharing)		 Allows you to export or share recipes and grocery lists with other apps on your device. Triggered only when you tap Share/Export. You control where the exported file is sent.

4. Local Storage and Data Retention

All recipes, ingredients, cooking notes, and grocery lists are stored locally in the app's private SQLite database on your device. Uninstalling MadeWithLove permanently deletes all locally stored app data.

5. Children's Privacy

MadeWithLove does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.
