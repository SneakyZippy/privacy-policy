PowerLab

Package ID: com.sneakyzippy.powerlab
Android battery telemetry and idle drain diagnostics tool (formerly PowerTrace).

Effective Date: November 2026 Application Version: 1.1+ Developer: SneakyZippy Source Code: GitHub Repository

Summary: PowerLab operates 100% offline (android.permission.INTERNET is not declared). All battery telemetry, alarms, and wakelock statistics remain entirely on your device in a local Room database. PowerLab contains zero trackers or advertisements.

1. Overview

PowerLab provides technical insights into what drains your device's battery while the screen is idle. It captures snapshots of Android's internal diagnostic sources (such as dumpsys batterystats, dumpsys alarm, and dumpsys power) to compute deltas between user-defined recording sessions (e.g., overnight drain) or continuous background monitoring.

2. Information Collection and Telemetry Policy

PowerLab is an offline diagnostic utility:

  • No Internet Access: PowerLab does not declare INTERNET permission and cannot transmit your battery statistics, hardware identifiers, or usage logs to any remote server.
  • No Analytics SDKs: There are no third-party trackers, crash telemetry suites (like Firebase or Crashlytics), or analytics beacons embedded in the application.
  • No Accounts: You are not required to create an account or provide any personal information.
  • No Ads: PowerLab is completely ad-free.

3. Diagnostic Permissions & Data Accessed

To perform deep battery diagnostics without requiring root privileges, PowerLab requests specific advanced Android diagnostic permissions (typically granted via ADB or optionally via Shizuku on-device):

Permission / API Diagnostic Purpose Data Scope & Handling
BATTERY_STATS Reads system-level battery state, discharge rate, and per-app hardware energy attribution tables. Saved locally to the app's internal database for user-configured recording sessions. Never uploaded.
DUMP Accesses internal diagnostic service dumps (e.g., dumpsys alarm, dumpsys power) to pinpoint partial wakelocks and background wakeup alarms. Parsed locally in real-time to compute energy consumption metrics. Raw dump logs are discarded after delta calculation unless explicitly exported by the user.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Correlates foreground app activity with background battery draw to identify idle drain culprits. Processed purely on-device for session delta reporting.
INTERACT_ACROSS_USERS Distinguishes multi-user and work-profile processes from system services in diagnostic logs. Internal UID mapping only.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE		 Maintains active manual benchmark sessions or user-controlled continuous monitoring intervals while the screen is off. Displays a clear ongoing notification in the status bar while recording is active.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers session status alerts and completion summaries. Local notifications only.
WAKE_LOCK Ensures diagnostic delta snapshots are cleanly calculated when baseline or stop triggers fire. Released immediately once calculations finish.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Resumes user-configured continuous monitoring schedules after device reboot or app update (optional feature). Local schedule restoration only.
<queries>
(Package Visibility & Shizuku)		 Resolves Android UIDs from diagnostic dumps to friendly app names/icons via MAIN intent queries, and checks if moe.shizuku.privileged.api is installed for optional PC-free ADB permission setup. Used strictly for local UI display and optional Shizuku integration. Installed app lists are never stored externally or transmitted.

4. Local Storage and Data Retention

All session recordings, baseline snapshots, and diagnostic metrics are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database (managed via Android Room) in the application's private sandbox directory:

  • You can delete individual recording sessions or clear the entire history at any time from within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling PowerLab completely purges the local database and all associated diagnostic caches.

5. User-Initiated Data Export

PowerLab includes a diagnostic export feature (using Android's FileProvider) allowing users to export session snapshots as HTML or CSV reports for personal debugging or bug reports.

This export is 100% initiated by you using Android's system file picker or share sheet. PowerLab never automatically uploads or shares exported reports. You maintain full ownership and control over where exported files are saved or sent.

6. Children's Privacy

PowerLab does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
